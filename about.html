Our Company

Celefrank Nigeria Limited is a fully indigenous company incorporated under the company and Allied matters Act 1990. It has positioned itself as a one-stop shop for various businesses of sea and air freight services, cargo logistics, warehousing, import and export, manufacturers’ representative, general contractors, all maritime and ancillary matters. We are also engaged actively in trading, procurement, marketing and distribution of consignments including petroleum products.

Our culture of excellent customer-centricity is tailored towards providing customer satisfaction which stands us out in the industry.

Our Mission

To become the hallmark for creative innovation, a model to our competitors and to offer bespoke services to our clients at competitive price

Our Value

Integrity Commitment to service, Social responsibility, Professionalism, Responsiveness, and Ingenuity

Our Vision
