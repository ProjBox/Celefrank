Freight Forwarding & Maritime Services

We handle end-to-end cargo movement across sea and air channels. From customs clearance to ship agency, Celefrank ensures seamless and compliant freight delivery both locally and globally.

Our Sea Freight services include customs brokerage services, Tracking of the consignment, Warehousing, Vessel charter, Bulk/ break bulk shipment, full container load and less container load.

Our Air freight services Consolidated express Shipment, Warehousing, Worldwide Pickup and delivery Over flight and ground handling.

What We Deliver:
We handle all aspects of cargo movement by sea and air, serving as your single point of contact from origin to destination.

We deliver your consignments:

  • Door to door
  • Seaport to Seaport
  • Door to Airport/Seaport
  • Airport/Seaport to Door.

We also guarantee full on line tracking and email updates for every shipment.

The Celefrank Operational Advantage

Every cargo shipment faces three critical challenges: regulatory compliance, transit reliability, and cost predictability. For over 15 years, Celefrank has engineered solutions for these exact pain points through our end-to-end freight management system.

Our team of licensed customs brokers, shipping coordinators, and logistics specialists don't just process paperwork - we own the entire cargo movement process. From the moment your goods are loaded at origin to final delivery at destination.
