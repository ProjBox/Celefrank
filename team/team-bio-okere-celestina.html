MRS. CHINYERE OKERE

  • C.E.O and Founder of Celefrank Nig. Ltd

She is a revolutionary leader per excellence. Before founding Celefrank Nigeria Limited, she has worked with Tidemark and Allied Marine Services limited where she rose to the position of a manager.
She has over 21 years cognate experience in Shipping/Freight forwarding, and has executed a lot of projects in the oil & gas sector.

She has PGD, MSC and PhD in Shipping and transport Management, and has attended many seminars and symposia in the Logistics, Freight Forwarding and oil and Gas services.
She is a member of chartered institute of logistics (CILT) London and also a member of women in maritime Africa (WIMA). At present, she is the Chairman of the Association of Nigerian Customs Licensed Agents (ANCLA) oil and gas free zone chapter

She has been actively involved in top flight business management for close to two decades now. She has attended several courses, seminars and symposia both locally and internationally of which the most recent include:

  • World Customs Organization Conference( WCO) on strategic information management held in Brussels Belgium from 9th to 11th October 2019.
  • She was part of the team that went to Uganda for the 2018 World Conference on the Ease of Doing business
  • She attended a similar Conference in Abu-Dhabi, Dubai in June 2019. Etc.
